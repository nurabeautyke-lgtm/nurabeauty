Our story

NURA was born from a simple belief: for a woman, hair is never just hair. It is identity, status and confidence. Yet Kenya's wig market was crowded at the bottom and thin at the top — plenty of units, little trust, and almost no true luxury experience. We set out to change that.

Every NURA wig is sewn from verified human hair by hand, in our Nairobi atelier. We do not mass-produce. We craft, we guide, and we stand behind every unit with a written guarantee.
